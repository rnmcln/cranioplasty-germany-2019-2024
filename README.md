# Cranioplasty by reconstruction material in Germany, 2019–2024

Analysis code for a nationwide study of cranioplasty in Germany based on aggregate
hospital discharge data reported under §21 of the Hospital Remuneration Act
(Krankenhausentgeltgesetz, KHEntgG) and published through the InEK DatenBrowser.
The study describes national volumes, case mix, in-hospital mortality and coded
infection for three groups of reconstruction material over six data years.

## Summary

**Question.** How do autologous, simple alloplastic and computer-aided-design (CAD)
cranioplasty differ nationally in volume, case mix, in-hospital mortality and coded
infection?

**Data.** Discharge episodes with a cranioplasty procedure code (OPS 5-020 family) in
the annual §21 data deliveries for 2019–2024. The unit of analysis is the discharge
episode, not the patient.

| Group | OPS codes | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Autologous | 5-020.2 | Reinsertion of the patient's previously removed bone flap |
| Simple alloplastic | 5-020.70 | Simple alloplastic implant, predominantly bone cement (episodes without a CAD code) |
| CAD | 5-020.71, .72, .74, .75 | Prefabricated computer-aided-design implant |

The DatenBrowser counts episodes carrying at least one selected code. The alloplastic
total (any of 5-020.70/.71/.72/.74/.75) and CAD were queried directly; the simple
alloplastic group is their difference.

**Main results (2019–2024, pooled).**

| | Autologous | Simple alloplastic | CAD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discharge episodes | 13,903 | 19,341 | 6,849 |
| Change in annual volume, 2019 to 2024 | −32.0% | +17.4% | +14.9% |
| In-hospital mortality, % (95% CI) | 3.02 (2.75–3.32) | 2.47 (2.26–2.69) | 0.99 (0.78–1.26) |
| Post-procedural infection, T81.4, % (95% CI) | 1.67 (1.47–1.90) | 1.99 (1.80–2.20) | 3.64 (3.22–4.11) |
| Nervous-system implant infection, T85.72, % (95% CI) | 0.67 (0.55–0.82) | 0.32 (0.25–0.41) | 0.88 (0.68–1.13) |

The autologous share of cranial reconstruction fell from 43.2% (2019) to 30.7% (2024).
CAD episodes had the lowest in-hospital mortality in every year (autologous versus CAD
risk ratio 3.04, 95% CI 2.36–3.92) and the highest rate of coded post-procedural
infection (CAD versus autologous risk ratio 2.18, 95% CI 1.83–2.60). Mortality and
coded infection therefore rank the groups differently. The groups also differ in case
mix: autologous episodes have the longest stays, simple alloplastic episodes the
shortest stays and the lowest coded complexity. All comparisons are unadjusted, and
the results describe the patient populations treated with each material; they do not
estimate the effect of the material itself.

Annual counts agree with published national procedure statistics: autologous counts
are identical for 2019–2023 and CAD counts differ by 3–7 episodes per year
(Hirsch et al. 2026, Table 1; https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci16050444).

## Reproducing the analysis

The aggregate input data are not redistributed here (see `data/README.md`). After
placing the six input files in `data/input/`:

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt     # matplotlib; the statistics use the standard library only
python run_all.py
```

The pipeline has no stochastic steps; repeated runs give byte-identical results and
figures. `code/03_validate.py` exits with a non-zero status if any check fails.

Statistical methods: Wilson 95% confidence intervals for proportions; risk ratios with
log-method 95% confidence intervals; Pearson's chi-square test; Holm adjustment across
the three mortality comparisons; Cochran–Armitage test for linear trend in annual
mortality.
